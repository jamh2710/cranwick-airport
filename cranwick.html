Parking

Cranwick has Short Stay, Medium Stay and Long Stay parking.

Car park Maximum / intended stay Shuttle information
Short Stay Up to 3 days Direct terminal access
Medium Stay Up to 5 days Terminal access available
Long Stay 7 days or more Shuttle serves Terminal 2 first, then Terminal 1
Delayed flights: If a flight is delayed and arrives a day later than scheduled, the permitted parking period is extended accordingly.

Security

Cranwick security uses modern screening procedures designed to keep passenger processing efficient.

Liquids

Passengers can take liquids through security in containers up to 2 litres.

Screening

Passengers and baggage are screened before entering the departures area.

Updated rules

The previous smaller liquid-container restrictions were removed in June 2026.

European Entry/Exit System

Cranwick supports the European Entry/Exit System (EES), which digitally records relevant entries and exits for travellers covered by the system.

Digital records

Travel movements covered by EES are recorded digitally rather than relying only on passport stamps.

Border control

Automated border-control technology can be used where available.

European system

EES is part of Europe's wider external-border management system.
